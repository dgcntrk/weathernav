Privacy policy

Updated October 19, 2026

Your trip, your choice

WeatherNav is a road-weather planner from Doga Canturk. You can use it without an account. It has no advertising, tracking SDKs or in-app analytics. WeatherNav does not operate a server that stores your trips.

Online providers receive information needed to find places, plan routes and retrieve weather. Their handling of those requests is described below.

Location and routes

My location asks for permission while you use the app and requests a current location fix. WeatherNav does not continuously track your drive or request background location. You can decline permission and enter a starting point instead. Change permission at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → WeatherNav.

Place searches, route endpoints and stops are sent to Apple Maps. Road and weather-check coordinates, including your starting point, are sent to Open-Meteo to obtain elevation, hourly forecasts and historical weather. These coordinates can reveal precise locations. Requests also expose your network IP address to the provider.

Providers and retention

Apple processes Maps and location requests under its privacy terms. Open-Meteo says its free API may log IP addresses and requested geographical coordinates for maintenance, misuse prevention and troubleshooting, and deletes these logs after 90 days. Open-Meteo says the information is not linked to user identities or shared with third parties.

Canadian weather alerts come from Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC). WeatherNav downloads the national alert feed and matches it to your route on your iPhone; it does not send your route to ECCC. ECCC still receives the network request, including its IP address. Provider policies apply independently.

On your iPhone

Saved trips contain places, coordinates, stops and chosen times. Saved trips and your unit preference remain in this app’s local storage until you remove them or delete the app. They may be included in device backups managed by Apple and your backup settings.

WeatherNav keeps a disposable terrain cache, including coordinates and elevation, to speed up repeat trips. iOS can remove caches. Current route forecasts and historical summaries are held in memory; networking services may maintain their own temporary caches. WeatherNav does not provide a cloud account or sync saved trips between devices.

Your controls

Remove a saved drive from Saved drives with its delete action. Deleting the app removes its local saved trips, preferences and caches; offloading the app may preserve saved data. Manage any device backups separately in iPhone Settings.

Removing local data does not delete requests already retained by providers. Use the provider privacy links above for their contact details and any applicable data requests. If you send feedback or contact support, share only information you want to provide and avoid including home addresses or sensitive trip details.

The support website

WeatherNav’s public support and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit the website, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes. The website does not add analytics or tracking scripts. Website hosting is separate from the app’s routing and weather requests.
