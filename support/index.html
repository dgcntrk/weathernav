Plan your drive

Tap Where to? and choose a destination. My location is the default starting point; tap it to choose another place. Leave now starts with the current time. Choose Leave at or Arrive by to plan ahead, and add stops or breaks when needed.

Departure times use the starting point’s time zone. Arrival times use the destination’s time zone. Weather is matched to your estimated progress along the road.

Compare routes

When Apple Maps returns alternatives, choose a route to see its weather. WeatherNav checks each route separately and keeps completed results available while it checks the others. Serious weather concerns or matching Canadian warnings may trigger an extra detour search.

A lower-exposure route can still have dangerous conditions. Detour searches may not find a useful bypass. Check official road conditions and closures before leaving; WeatherNav does not provide turn-by-turn navigation or closure information.

Understand the outlook

Near-term trips use hourly weather forecasts. Later dates, up to 90 days ahead, use daily weather from five previous years around that time of year. Historical outlooks describe past conditions, not the weather on your future travel day.

Confidence reflects forecast lead time and available data. It is not a measured probability of being correct. Weather highlights summarize meaningful changes such as rain, snow, fog, strong gusts and temperature shifts; their times and locations are approximate.

Mountain roads

WeatherNav checks the terrain along your road and adds weather checks around detected high points and steep or elevated stretches. Open Mountain coverage for more detail.

Terrain-derived high points are not a complete list of mountain passes. Narrow crests, tunnels and lower passes can be missed, and local mountain weather can differ from a forecast model. Missing coverage is shown explicitly.

If a check is slow or unavailable

An internet connection is required for new searches, routes and forecasts. A first long trip can take several minutes because it checks many road and terrain locations. Completed weather remains usable while more checks load. You can cancel the check and try again later.

If the provider limits requests, wait before refreshing. Missing weather is unknown, never an all-clear. Saving a trip saves the plan; it does not download an offline forecast. Weather refreshes when you plan or tap refresh, not continuously as you drive.
